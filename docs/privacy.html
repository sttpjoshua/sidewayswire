Sideways Wire Publisher: Privacy Policy

Sideways Wire Publisher is an internal tool operated by STTP Media, Industrieweg 91, 7202 CA Zutphen, the Netherlands. It publishes content to social media accounts owned by STTP Media (@sidewayswire on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube).

What we store: access tokens for our own accounts (encrypted) and the IDs and publication dates of posts we published. We do not collect personal data of other users.

Public information: to report on drift events we read public posts and news from drift series, teams and drivers. We credit creators by name and tag, and do not store their personal data beyond the public link to the post.

Removal requests: rights holders can ask us to remove content via a direct message to @sidewayswire or by email to sidewayswire@outlook.com. We remove it as soon as possible.

Contact: sidewayswire@outlook.com
