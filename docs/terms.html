Sideways Wire Publisher: Terms of Service

Sideways Wire Publisher ("the app") is an internal publishing tool operated by STTP Media, Industrieweg 91, 7202 CA Zutphen, the Netherlands. It is used only by STTP Media to publish drift news, clips and graphics to the Sideways Wire accounts it owns on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube.

Who can use the app

The app is not offered to the public. Only STTP Media staff can sign in, and only with the Sideways Wire accounts. No other users can create an account.

Connecting a TikTok account

When the Sideways Wire TikTok account is connected, the app uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. It reads the basic profile of the connected account and publishes videos that a member of our team has reviewed. Before each post the team sees a preview, the caption, the privacy level and the interaction settings, and chooses them. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions.

Content

We credit creators, series, teams and drivers by name and tag. Rights holders can ask us to remove content through a direct message to @sidewayswire; we remove it as soon as possible. All published content must follow the community guidelines and terms of each platform.

No warranty

The app is provided as is, for internal use. STTP Media may change or stop the app at any time.
